Why EO4DES?

Desertification is a complex land degradation process driven by the interaction of climatic stress, anthropogenic pressure and environmental vulnerability. In Mediterranean regions, increasing drought frequency, water scarcity, soil degradation and vegetation stress require integrated monitoring strategies based on repeatable, scalable and scientifically robust observations.

EO4DES addresses this challenge by exploiting Earth Observation data and geospatial technologies to support the analysis of desertification-related processes in Southern Italy. The project focuses on the definition of relevant environmental variables, the use of multi-source satellite datasets, the development of automated open-source workflows and the implementation of WebGIS tools for dissemination and decision support.

Project Objectives

Environmental Variables

Definition of the most relevant variables affecting desertification processes in urban, coastal and agricultural contexts.

Earth Observation Data

Exploration of geospatial big data and new satellite missions for the assessment of land degradation dynamics.

Open-source Workflows

Development of automatic tools based on open-source and cloud computing platforms for large-scale environmental analysis.

WebGIS Platform

Implementation of an interactive WebGIS environment to disseminate project outcomes and support end-users' decisions.

Scientific Framework

The concept of desertification has evolved from the simple expansion of sandy deserts to a broader interpretation of land degradation in arid, semi-arid and dry sub-humid areas. International frameworks such as UNCCD, MEDALUS and the World Atlas of Desertification have progressively contributed to the definition of indicators and assessment approaches. EO4DES builds on this background by integrating remote sensing, environmental indicators and local-scale geospatial analyses.

1996 UNCCD definition of desertification as land degradation in vulnerable climatic regions.
1998 MEDALUS framework for Environmentally Sensitive Areas assessment.
2012 DESIRE project contributions to global soil degradation and desertification studies.
2019 World Atlas of Desertification as a reference framework for vulnerability assessment.
2025 EO4DES project: local-scale, EO-based monitoring in Southern Italy.
