EO4DES Case Study: Lecce (Salento Peninsula, Italy)

The Salento Peninsula, located in the Apulia Region (Southern Italy), represents one of the Mediterranean areas most vulnerable to land degradation and desertification processes. Characterized by a typical Mediterranean climate (Köppen–Geiger classification: Csa), the region experiences hot, dry summers and mild, wetter winters, with increasing trends in temperature and drought frequency associated with climate change.

The EO4DES case study focuses on the municipality of Lecce and its surrounding territory, a heterogeneous landscape where urban settlements coexist with extensive agricultural areas dominated by olive groves, vineyards, and non-irrigated croplands. The area has been increasingly affected by water scarcity, soil degradation, vegetation stress, and the combined impacts of climate variability and land-use pressures.

Within the EO4DES project, the Salento case study serves as a testbed for the development and application of Earth Observation-based methodologies aimed at monitoring environmental conditions related to desertification risk. Multi-source geospatial datasets, including satellite imagery and ancillary environmental data, are used to characterize land surface dynamics, vegetation conditions, and environmental stress factors at different spatial and temporal scales.

The activities carried out in the study area contribute to evaluating the potential of Earth Observation technologies for supporting the assessment of land degradation processes and providing evidence-based information for sustainable land management and environmental decision-making. The results obtained from the Salento case study will support the development of transferable methodologies applicable to other Mediterranean regions facing similar environmental challenges.
